Module Installation 

1- Download the relevant version file and un-zip it.

2- Copy the unzipped folder to your Odoo add-ons directory

3- Restart Odoo services

4- Activate Developer mode

5- Open the Apps menu and click on the top bar ‘Update Apps List’.

6- Search for Teya module ⇒ activate it.

Configuration for Teya Payment 

1- Click Point of Sale ⇒ “Configuration”  ⇒ “Payment Methods”.

2- Click “New” button on the top left to create a new payment method.

3- After filling payment details e.g;

Method : Card Payment

Journal: Choose Bank or pick suitable option

On right side next to “use a Payment terminal”: select Teya from the drop-down menu.

4- Click on the "Generate Token" button above,

a new verify device page is displayed, scan the QR using mobile camera or click on the Verification Uri Link

5- Teya Code page will be displayed ⇒ press "submit", you will need to enter your merchant email and password

6- A new page will show, then click "confirm", after this stage.

7- Then go back to the "verify device" page and press "Verify"

8- Then click on the "Get Store" button.

9- Click the drop down menu in front of the "Store" to choose your shop.

10- Save these settings.

POS configuration:

1- Click Point of Sale, then click on “Configuration” and choose “Settings”.

2- Scroll down to the "Payment terminals" and select Teya by activating the tick-box

3- A drop-down menu will show below Teya, pick your Terminal device from the list

4- Save these settings

Final notes

Once all these steps are completed, you will be ready to use your Point of sale with Teya fully integrated.

"Important" To make any changes or settings for POS payment, the POS session must be closed.
